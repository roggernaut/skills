# Clean AI writing tells

## Workflow

1. Read [references/patterns.md](references/patterns.md) before drafting or revising substantial copy.
2. Identify the intended audience, register, house style and claim the text must make.
3. Fix substance before phrasing. Remove hollow claims, add concrete evidence and decide what the writer actually believes.
4. Rewrite with plain verbs, direct sentence shapes, varied rhythm and thesis-first structure.
5. Run the reference's revision pass after drafting.
6. Read the result aloud mentally. Remove any sentence whose polish exceeds its content.

## Hard defaults

- Do not use em dashes.
- Do not use the Oxford comma. Write "red, white and blue," not "red, white, and blue."
- Do not use negative parallelism such as “not X, but Y.” State the positive claim.
- Do not use rhetorical fragments for punch.
- Lead with the thesis or useful fact.
- Prefer specific behavior and verified numbers over claims of importance.
- Give direct criticism without a praise sandwich or diplomatic padding.
- Preserve meaning, factual qualifications, brand terminology and the author's real voice.

Treat the catalog as diagnostic guidance. A single listed word is not proof of anything, and a surface cleanup is insufficient when the argument remains generic or empty.
