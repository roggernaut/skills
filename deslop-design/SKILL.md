# Clean AI design tells

## Workflow

1. Read [references/patterns.md](references/patterns.md) before designing or reviewing a public-facing interface.
2. Establish the product, audience, brand constraints, real content and conversion or usability goal.
3. Inspect the existing design system and reuse its deliberate choices before introducing new ones.
4. Fix substance first. Replace decorative stand-ins with product UI, real data, verified proof or useful interaction.
5. Establish hierarchy and vary layout according to content weight. Avoid repeated symmetrical templates.
6. Choose a small set of brand-specific decisions for type, color, spacing, radius, imagery and motion.
7. Apply the graded fixes in the reference at a level appropriate to the surface and audience.
8. Run the reference's revision pass and verify accessibility, responsiveness, performance and reduced-motion behavior.
9. Invoke `deslop-writing` for visible marketing or product copy when it is available.

## Hard defaults

- Do not add decoration merely to make a sparse layout feel designed.
- Do not use icons that repeat neighboring text.
- Reserve cards for content that needs containment.
- Avoid uniform section rhythms and repeated three-column feature grids.
- Use real product evidence before conceptual illustrations.
- Preserve useful conventions and accessibility. Do not over-correct into affectation.

Treat the catalog as diagnostic guidance. Any single component can be fine; the tell comes from the combination, the repetition and the lack of product specificity.
